1

Collect

Public lists of malicious IP addresses (botnets, scanners, brute-force, C&C servers…) are fetched several times a day. Each list has a weight reflecting how reliable and severe it is.

2

Map

Every IP is matched with the prefix and the autonomous system that announced it in BGP on that day (CAIDA data, through IP ASN History). IPs not announced by anyone (AS0) are ignored.

3

Rank

For each list, the malicious IPs of an AS are multiplied by the list weight and divided by the number of IPs the AS announces. The ranks of all lists are then added up.

AS rank (per list)
malicious IPs in the AS × weight of the listIPs announced by the AS
Prefix rank
malicious IPs in the prefix × weight of the listsize of the prefix
Country rank
sum of the ranks of the ASes routed in the country 
  • Global rank of an AS = sum of its ranks over all the selected lists. Filtering sources recomputes it with those lists only.
  • Reading it: 0 means nothing was reported. Because the count is divided by the size of the AS, a small network with a few bad IPs can rank higher than a large one with many.
  • IPv4 and IPv6 are ranked separately. The ranking runs every hour for the current day (and the previous day before noon).
